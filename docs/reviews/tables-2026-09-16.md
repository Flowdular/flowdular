# Tables at any width, 2026-09-16

## Scope and evidence

The owner's screenshots of Audit and Modules showed cells breaking inside
words ("admin@example.co / m", "7:39 / PM"), cut tags, ids wrapping over three
lines and cut buttons. The fix is platform wide: typed cells in
`@flowdular/ui` that never break a word, column priorities that hide in a
narrow card and list the hidden columns under the row, row actions that fold
into a More menu, and a keyboard way into clickable rows. The rules are in
`docs/design-system.md`, section Tables.

Nobody could open the signed-in app in this session. The readings below come
from a render harness instead: the real `Table` and cell components rendered
in jsdom with sample rows shaped like the audit and module endpoints (an email
over 50 characters, a UUID subject, a 22 character action code, a module with
four dependents), written out as HTML with the shipped stylesheet and the IBM
Plex fonts, and captured with headless Chrome. "Before" used `Table.tsrx` and
the stylesheets from `main` at e8ae391; "after" used this branch. Widths are
the width of the workspace (the window minus the sidebar and the context rail).
Inside it the table is 1142 px wide at 1200, 842 px at 900 and 566 px at 600,
where the shell's narrow layout pads 16 px instead of 28.

Not verified in a browser: the More menu's placement and focus (covered by
jsdom tests only), the copy button on hover, live data and real translations.

## Audit

### 1200 px workspace

Before: all five columns share the width by percentage. Time reads
"Sep 16, 2026, 7:39 PM" on one line, but a long actor breaks mid word
("owner.with.a.long.addres / s@northwind- / traders.example.com") and a UUID
subject wraps over two lines. Details are small text beside everything else.

After: all five columns show. Time is a fixed 170 px column that reads
"Sep 16, 7:39 PM" (the year only for another year) with the full timestamp and
seconds as its title. The long actor ends in an ellipsis on one line and
keeps the address as its title. The action tag shows its whole code. The
subject reads the type over a shortened id ("4783b5b2…40d8") with a copy
button on row hover. Details wrap at spaces, two lines at most. Every row is
the same two-line height.

### 900 px workspace

Before: columns squeeze to percentages of 842 px. Time wraps to two lines,
emails break mid word ("user@example.c / om"), action tags are cut
("auth.sign-in.succ", "users.member.remo") and the long actor spreads over
five lines. Rows range from two to five lines.

After: time, actor, action and subject show; details hide. Each row starts
with an expand button. Emails, tags and times stay whole on one line (the
55 character email ends in an ellipsis). Expanding a row adds one "Details"
line under it with the metadata in full.

### 600 px workspace

Before: the table keeps its 640 px minimum inside a 566 px card and scrolls
sideways. The details column is cut at the card edge, emails and timestamps
break mid word, and tags are cut to "auth.sign-i".

After: time, actor and action show and fit without scrolling. Subject and
details hide. An expanded row lists Subject (the type over the shortened id)
and Details as label and value pairs under the row. Nothing is cut except the
long email, which ends in an ellipsis with its title.

## Modules

### 1200 px workspace

Before: all six columns and the two action buttons show. The module column is
about 140 px, so "Authentication and workspaces" wraps to two lines and
capability lists end in ellipses. Nothing breaks mid word at this width.

After: module, version, dependents, status and the Open and Deactivate buttons
show. Platform and permissions hide, because beside them the module column
would drop under its reading width. The module name and id each keep one line.
An expanded row lists Platform (surfaces over the capability list) and
Permissions.

### 900 px workspace

Before: the table hits its minimum width and scrolls sideways. Dependents and
status sit under the sticky action column and are not readable without
scrolling, and "notifications.core" runs into the version column.

After: module, status and both action buttons show, with no scrolling. Version,
platform, permissions and dependents hide; an expanded row lists all four, the
version with its spec version and the dependents with their summary.

### 600 px workspace

Before: only module, version and a cut platform column are visible beside the
sticky buttons; the rest needs sideways scrolling and module names wrap to
three lines.

After: module and status show, and the two actions fold into one More button
at the end of the row. The menu lists Open and Deactivate; a refused
Deactivate stays in the menu with its reason. An expanded row lists the four
hidden columns. No scrolling.

## Rules adopted

- A cell is a typed cell: `CellText`, `CellStack`, `CellTime`, `CellCode`,
  `CellTag`, `CellNumber` or `CellMuted`. Inside a table text wraps only at
  spaces; a value without spaces ends in an ellipsis with its value as title.
- `priority` 1 is identity, state and the main value; 2 secondary ids, counts
  and timestamps; 3 details and descriptions. The first column is always 1.
- A priority hides when the card is narrower than the columns that would stay
  beside it (px widths as declared, 200 px per flexible column, 28 px for the
  expand button), rounded up to a 40 px step between 480 and 1600 px. The table
  scrolls only below priority 1 at 120 px per flexible column. Container
  queries decide; nothing is measured by script.
- Two row actions fold into More below three times the action column; more
  than two are always in the menu.
- A clickable row opens from a button in its first cell.
- The shell provides the copy (en and pl) and the locale through
  `TableLocaleContext`.

## Migration

Every table definition outside the streams named below moved to the typed
cells with px widths, one `auto` column and priorities: access, agents,
approvals, audit, auth, automations, connectors, directory, exports, import,
metering, notifications, profile, sandbox, search, system, users and workflows
under `modules/`, and the session picker in `packages/sandbox`. Three screens
now lead with the record's identity instead of its state: Approvals and the
notification inbox put the title first, and legal holds put the scope and
status first. Formatting helpers the cells replaced were removed with their
assertions. Each module is patch bumped; the platform API is 0.1.19.

## Follow-up: cell gaps, documents, research and adapters

The three cell gaps are closed with additive props. `CellText` takes `muted`
for a description in the secondary ink (`ui-cell-text--muted`). `CellNumber`
takes a string `value` that arrives formatted (`1.2K`, `<$0.01`, `3/5`) and
shows it as it came, ignoring `options`. `CellMuted` takes a `title`. Each has a
render test in `packages/ui/tests/cells-render.test.tsrx` that fails against
the previous cells. The platform API stays 0.1.19: the snapshot does not emit
declarations for `.tsrx` files, so cell props are outside
`pnpm platform-api:check` today.

Every table in `modules/documents/src/client`, `modules/research/src/client`
and `modules/adapters/src/client` now uses the typed cells with px widths, one
`auto` column and priorities. Step classes below come from `tableLayout` with
the declared widths.

- Documents: file (name over upload time) and scan stay; owner module and
  record hide next, type, size and uploader first. The size is a
  `CellNumber` string from `byteLabel`. A manager's three actions fold into
  More: p3 hides below 1240 px, p2 below 840 px.
- Research evidence and queries now lead with the source and the query,
  since the first cell opens the drawer. Evidence: retrieved and run hide
  below 600 px, the digest (a `CellCode` in place of `shortDigest`) below
  720 px. Queries: query, adapter and results stay; searched and cost hide
  below 760 px, caller below 840 px.
- Query attempts (720 px drawer): adapter over time, outcome and the error
  stay; attempt and duration hide below 760 px, so they sit in the expanded
  row inside the drawer.
- Adapters list: adapter over module and the last run (status over time)
  stay. A `CellStack` line cannot hold text and a `<time>` on one line, so the
  last run's row counts moved to a new priority 3 column, "Last run rows"
  ("Wiersze ostatniego przebiegu"). For a manager p3 hides below 1320 px and
  p2 (direction, target, schedule) below 960 px.
- Adapter runs (720 px drawer): queued over trigger and status over the error
  stay. Read, created or pushed and failed are priority 2, pages, updated and
  skipped priority 3. Two row actions at 220 px leave no room for a count
  beside them, so in the drawer every count is in the expanded row. The run
  rows keep row, outcome and reason; key and field hide below 800 px. The dry
  run preview keeps all three columns.

Removed: research `shortDigest` and its re-export from the client entry; no
test asserted it and nothing else imported it. `timestampLabel`, `byteLabel`,
`countLabel`, `numberLabel` and `durationLabel` stay because drawers, forms
and messages still use them. Versions: documents 0.2.1, research 0.2.2,
adapters 0.1.1.

A throwaway jsdom render (not committed) mounted all seven tables with
stubbed API answers: every cell rendered, the step classes matched the numbers
above, and an expanded run listed its hidden counts. Not verified in a browser,
so container queries, real copy and the drawers at 720 px were not seen.
